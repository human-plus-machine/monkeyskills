Apply each heading to the target. Definitions: [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md). Skill **invocation** flags: [SKILL-MECHANICS.md](SKILL-MECHANICS.md).

For every heading: change the target, or write N/A and why.

## Context pointers

Front-load a **leading word**. One trigger per **branch**. Cut identity the body already carries. Sharpen a weak pointer before inlining the target.

## The two loads

Pay **context load** only for always-on pointers. Pay **cognitive load** where a human should be the index.

## Information hierarchy

Put **steps** first. Keep in-file **reference** that every **branch** needs. Disclose the rest. **Co-locate** a concept's rules with its definition. Cut **sprawl** by disclosing or splitting.

## Steps and completion criteria

End every **step** on a checkable **completion criterion**. Raise **demand** until **legwork** is exhaustive. Sharpen a fuzzy bound before hiding **post-completion steps**.

## When to split

Split by sequence only to stop **premature completion**. Split by **invocation** only on the skill branch (SKILL-MECHANICS.md). Agent-doc branch: N/A for invocation splits.

## Leading words

Prefer a pretrained token. Repeat the token, not the paragraph. Prompt the **positive**; pair any hard **negation** with the target behaviour.

## Pruning

One **single source of truth** per meaning. Leave **environment** facts in the environment unless lookup is expensive. Delete **sediment** and **no-ops** (the whole sentence).
