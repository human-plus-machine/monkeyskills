- All required inputs are sourced, measured, labeled assumptions, or blockers.
- Every formula has units and internally consistent substitutions.

- Every in-scope component has a deep-dive row.
- Full HLD has a council winner in `council/synthesis.md`; losers are Rejected
  alternatives.

  cloud map is sourced or TBD.

- Failure handling preserves stated invariants.
- Security and tenancy claims are concrete.
- Rejected alternatives are materially viable options, not straw men.
- At least one accepted failure and one measurable kill condition exist.
